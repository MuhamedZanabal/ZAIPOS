# Bahrain product baseline

ZAIPOS is Bahrain-native by default.

- Product identity: ZAIPOS
- User-facing language: English
- Locale: `en-BH`
- Currency: BHD
- Money display precision: three decimal places
- Standard VAT default: 10%, with zero-rated/exempt treatment supported by configured tax rules
- Telephone country code: +973
- Local payment terminology: Cash, Card, BenefitPay, Bank Transfer
- Bahrain digital-order terminology: Talabat, WhatsApp, In-house Delivery

Compatibility-only database storage values may remain where changing persisted enums would break historical data. They must not be exposed as active foreign-market integrations in the UI or runtime.
